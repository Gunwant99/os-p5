🥖 Bakery: Producer-Consumer Problem

Bakers (producers) fill the shelf. Customers (consumers) empty it. Semaphores keep them in sync.

👨‍🍳 Bakers (producers)

Display Shelf (buffer)

5empty (free slots)

🛍️ Customers (consumers)

Rule: wait(empty) → wait(mutex) → change shelf → signal(mutex) → signal(full). Consumers do the reverse.
